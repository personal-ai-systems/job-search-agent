# Career Evidence And Tailoring

Retrieve the current CV, the relevant interview/checkpoint and original evidence before adding claims. Indexes help locate evidence; they do not override newer user corrections. Keep detailed career facts outside the portable skill.

## Evidence Mapping

For each material addition, distinguish:

- context and business objective;
- options reviewed versus recommendations versus decisions owned;
- scope (individual solution, shared platform, practice, enterprise);
- governance and actual decision authority;
- planned, partially delivered or achieved outcome;
- metric units, period and contribution versus sole causality.

Use enterprise-architecture language only where this mapping supports it: current/target state, transition roadmap, capability fit, reference architecture, governance, decision records and data ownership. Do not rename historical employment titles or imply formal enterprise-wide ownership from solution participation. A TOGAF-like approach does not establish certification or a formally adopted TOGAF practice.

Do not turn proposed savings into realized savings, opportunities into won revenue, an inherited-platform recovery into invention, partial migration into completion, or a three-year figure into an annual figure. Separate personal AI research/projects from employer production deployments. Respect expressly excluded examples for a particular audience.

Position the Profile around the requested professional scope. Put detailed achievements and financial metrics in Highlights/experience unless the approved selected variant deliberately differs. Preserve approved tone in that variant; do not import an old joke or phrase into every role.

## Questions And Changes

Honor a requested interview format/count, but retrieve already answered questions before asking again. Otherwise ask one focused question only when a missing fact changes a material claim; prepare independent supported edits while waiting. Never infer years of tool experience, certification, salary, availability, citizenship or eligibility.

Keep formatting-only and export-only changes free from content rewriting. For tailoring, maintain a short provenance/change record outside the resume. Before finalization, compare names, titles, dates, claims, metrics and reading order against the chosen source, explaining intentional changes rather than silently treating every difference as an improvement.
