# Match Evidence To The Role

Build a brief requirement-to-evidence map from the employer's actual description: leadership scope, technology, outcomes, work model, eligibility and requested documents. Distinguish demonstrated, adjacent and unverified experience. A requested keyword is not evidence; do not invent skills to maximize a match.

Use the role-appropriate verified track: Adobe/AEM for Adobe delivery, Engineering Manager for people/platform leadership, Full Stack for hands-on development, Enterprise Architect for evidenced strategy/governance, or a supported targeted derivative. Do not assume an architecture CV is best for every senior role or an Engineering Manager CV for every Adobe role. Explain a material mismatch and ask only the missing question needed to resolve it.

When tailoring is authorized, invoke resume-update and follow its native-source and visual QA requirements. Preserve photograph proportions, page-edge section design, fonts, chevrons, user spacing and original files. No screenshot/PDF-only repair with an inconsistent editable master. Reuse the current role-appropriate source, not an old attachment or another agent's changing copy. Read-only snapshots are appropriate while another owner edits.

Observe page/word/file limits. Record filename/path/hash and prove upload by actual attachment readback. Native save/export proof, Drive backup proof, external display and employer upload are distinct. A PDF timestamp does not identify what the employer received. Do not claim employer-byte verification without download/readback evidence.

For letters and screening narratives, use specific supported examples, accurate ownership and measurable outcomes. Separate personal AI experimentation from employer deployments, proposals from delivered systems, cumulative savings from annual savings, and a phased partial transition from completed migration. Never include assistant notes, drafting status or authorization language in applicant-facing documents.

Fill every requested recruiter table row as carefully as a web form. Keep the answer in the existing thread; inspect Sent/drafts before another email. A new email is not automatically the right repair for an earlier omission. Sending email or recruiter outreach requires its own applicable approval.
